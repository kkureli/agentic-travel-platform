Antalya is one of the most popular tourism destinations in Türkiye.
The city is known for Mediterranean beaches and resort hotels.
Belek contains many luxury resorts designed for families.
Many hotels in the region provide pools, children's clubs, and private beaches.

Standard hotel reservations may be cancelled without penalty up to 48 hours before check-in.
Late cancellations may result in the first night's accommodation fee.
Non-refundable reservations normally cannot be refunded.

Travel insurance may protect travellers from certain unexpected events.
Some policies provide emergency medical coverage during a trip.
Coverage limits depend on the insurance product selected.
