# Travel Insurance Exclusions

## General Exclusions
Travel insurance does not cover every cancelled or interrupted trip. Coverage depends on the purchased policy, the reason for the claim, and supporting documentation.

## Known Events
Losses caused by events already known or reasonably foreseeable before insurance was purchased may be excluded.

## Change of Mind
Cancelling simply because the traveler no longer wishes to travel is normally not covered unless the policy specifically includes such protection.

## Missing Documentation
Claims may be rejected when required documentation cannot be provided.

## Hotel Cancellation Rules
Travel insurance coverage is separate from the hotel's cancellation policy. A non-refundable reservation may still be reimbursed by an eligible insurance claim, while insurance does not automatically make the hotel reservation refundable.
