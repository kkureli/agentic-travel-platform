# Customer Support Escalation

## Standard Support Review
Customer support can assist with reservation questions, payment issues, cancellation requests, and refund status inquiries.

## Exceptional Requests
When a customer requests an exception to a standard policy, support may escalate the case for additional review. Examples include exceptional medical situations, hotel service failures, or unusual reservation problems.

## Required Evidence
Customers may be asked to provide documents, photographs, medical records, payment records, or other evidence relevant to the request.

## No Guarantee of Approval
Escalation does not guarantee that a refund, credit, or policy exception will be approved.

## Resolution
The customer is informed when the review has been completed and any approved action has been processed.
