# Hotel No-Show Policy

## Definition of a No-Show
A reservation is considered a no-show when the guest does not arrive on the scheduled check-in date and has not cancelled within the applicable cancellation period.

## Standard Reservations
The hotel may charge the first night's accommodation fee when the guest does not arrive and has not cancelled in time. Remaining nights may be released.

## Non-Refundable Reservations
For non-refundable reservations, the full reservation amount may remain non-refundable when the guest does not arrive. A no-show does not normally create an automatic right to a refund.

## Exceptional Circumstances
Guests who miss check-in because of exceptional circumstances may contact customer support and request a review. A review does not guarantee a refund and may require supporting documentation.

## Late Arrival
Guests expecting to arrive after normal check-in hours should contact the hotel or travel platform in advance. Failure to notify the property may cause the reservation to be treated as a no-show.
