# Hotel Check-In Policy

## Check-In Time
Check-in times vary by property and are displayed in the reservation information. Early arrival does not guarantee immediate room access.

## Identification
Guests may be required to present valid identification. The reservation name should match the guest information provided during booking.

## Payment at Property
Hotels may request outstanding balances, deposits, local taxes, or incidental charges during check-in.

## Late Check-In
Guests arriving late should notify the hotel in advance when possible. Without notice, some hotels may release the room or treat the reservation as a no-show.

## Reservation Confirmation
Guests should keep their confirmation number available. Check-in requirements are separate from refund and cancellation eligibility.
