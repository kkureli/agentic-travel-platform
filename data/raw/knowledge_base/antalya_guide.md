# Antalya Travel Guide

Antalya is a major Mediterranean travel destination in Türkiye known for beaches, historical sites, and resort hotels.

## Lara

Lara is known for large resort hotels and its proximity to Antalya Airport.

The area is popular with travellers looking for all-inclusive accommodation and beach access.

## Belek

Belek is well known for luxury resorts, golf facilities, and family-oriented hotels.

Many resorts in Belek offer pools, children's clubs, sports facilities, and private beach access.

## Kaleici

Kaleici is Antalya's historic old town.

Visitors can explore narrow streets, historical buildings, restaurants, shops, and the old harbour.
