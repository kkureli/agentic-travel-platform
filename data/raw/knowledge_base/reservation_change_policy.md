# Reservation Change Policy

## Change Requests
Customers may request changes to check-in dates, check-out dates, guest information, and room type. Changes are subject to availability and the original reservation conditions.

## Price Differences
If the modified reservation is more expensive, the customer may need to pay the difference. A lower price does not automatically guarantee a refund of the difference.

## Non-Refundable Reservations
A non-refundable reservation may also be non-changeable. Some hotels may permit changes as an exception, but approval is not guaranteed.

## Cancellation vs Modification
Changing a reservation is different from cancelling it. Cancelling and creating a new booking may trigger the cancellation rules of the original reservation.

## Hotel Approval
Certain modifications require confirmation from the hotel before they become effective.
