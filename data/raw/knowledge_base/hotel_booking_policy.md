# Hotel Booking Policy

## Reservation Confirmation

A hotel reservation becomes confirmed after the payment process is completed and a confirmation number is issued.

Customers receive the reservation details through email and the travel platform.

## Guest Information

Customers must provide accurate guest names and contact information when making a reservation.

Some hotels may require identification during check-in.

## Reservation Changes

Requests to change check-in dates, guest information, or room type are subject to hotel availability and pricing rules.

A reservation change may result in a price difference.
