# Loyalty Program

Members of the travel platform's loyalty program can earn points from eligible reservations.

## Earning Points

Points are calculated based on eligible booking amounts and promotional campaigns.

Some discounted products may not qualify for points.

## Using Points

Customers may use available points toward eligible hotel and travel reservations.

Points cannot normally be converted directly into cash.

## Membership Levels

Customers may receive different membership levels based on their travel activity.

Higher membership levels may include additional campaigns, discounts, or support benefits.
