# Travel Insurance

Travel insurance may provide protection against certain unexpected events occurring before or during a trip.

## Trip Cancellation

Eligible cancellation reasons may include serious illness, injury, or other covered events defined by the insurance policy.

## Medical Coverage

Some insurance products cover emergency medical expenses incurred while travelling.

Coverage limits and exclusions depend on the selected insurance product.

## Lost Baggage

Certain plans may compensate customers for baggage that is lost or significantly delayed during transportation.

Customers must provide the documents required by the insurer when making a claim.
