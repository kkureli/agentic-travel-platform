# Loyalty Program Refund Rules

## Points Earned on Refunded Reservations
Loyalty points are generally awarded only for eligible completed travel. If a reservation is fully refunded, points awarded for that reservation may be removed.

## Partial Refunds
For a partially refunded reservation, points may be recalculated based on the final eligible booking amount.

## Bookings Paid With Points
When an eligible booking paid with points is cancelled, the return of points depends on reservation conditions and loyalty rules. A non-refundable hotel rate does not automatically become refundable because points were used.

## Promotional Points
Promotional or bonus points may have separate expiration and refund rules.

## Cash Refunds
Loyalty membership does not itself create a right to a cash refund for a non-refundable hotel reservation.
