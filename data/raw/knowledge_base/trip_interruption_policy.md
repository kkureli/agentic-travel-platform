# Trip Interruption Policy

## Trip Interruption
Trip interruption occurs after a traveler has already started a trip but must end it early because of an eligible event. This differs from trip cancellation, which occurs before departure.

## Potential Coverage
Depending on the insurance policy, eligible expenses may include unused prepaid travel costs and certain additional transportation expenses.

## Illness During Travel
Unexpected illness or injury during the trip may qualify when policy conditions are satisfied. Medical documentation may be required.

## Hotel Stays
Unused hotel nights may be included in an eligible insurance claim, but reimbursement is determined by the insurance provider. The hotel's own refund policy remains separate.

## Claim Review
Claims are subject to the terms, limits, exclusions, and documentation requirements of the purchased insurance policy.
