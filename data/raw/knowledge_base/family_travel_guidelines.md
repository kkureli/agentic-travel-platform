# Family Travel Guidelines

## Family-Friendly Hotels
Families may consider hotels with family rooms, children's pools, supervised activities, and easy access to beaches or attractions.

## Antalya Regions
Areas such as Lara, Belek, and Side include many family-oriented resorts. The best location depends on holiday type, transfer time, beach preference, and planned activities.

## Child Policies
Hotels may apply different rules for children depending on age, room occupancy, meal plans, and extra beds.

## Identification
Some properties may request identification for all guests, including children, during check-in.

## Cancellation
Family travel status does not change reservation cancellation conditions. Refund eligibility remains subject to the selected rate and applicable policy.
