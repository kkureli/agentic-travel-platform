# Payment and Charge Policy

## Reservation Payments
Hotel reservations may require full payment, partial payment, or payment at the property depending on the selected rate and hotel conditions.

## Authorization Holds
Some payment methods may show a temporary authorization hold before the final charge is completed. An authorization hold is not necessarily a completed payment.

## Duplicate Charges
Customers who believe they were charged more than once should contact customer support with the reservation number and payment details.

## Currency Conversion
Banks or card providers may apply currency conversion or international transaction fees. These charges are not hotel cancellation penalties.

## Refunds
When a refund is approved under the applicable reservation policy, it is generally returned to the original payment method. Payment processing rules do not determine refund eligibility.
