# Hotel Cancellation Policy

## Standard Reservations

Standard hotel reservations can be cancelled without penalty up to 48 hours before the scheduled check-in time.

Cancellations made less than 48 hours before check-in may be charged the first night's accommodation fee.

## Non-Refundable Reservations

Reservations marked as non-refundable cannot normally be cancelled for a refund.

In exceptional circumstances, customers may contact customer support and request a review.

## Hotel-Initiated Cancellation

If a hotel cancels a confirmed reservation, the customer will receive a full refund.

The travel platform may also assist the customer in finding alternative accommodation with similar characteristics.
