# Hotel Refund Processing

## Approved Refunds
When a hotel refund has been approved, the travel platform submits the refund to the original payment method whenever possible.

## Processing Time
Refunds are normally submitted within several business days after approval. The time required for the amount to appear depends on the bank, card network, payment provider, and original payment method.

## Partial Refunds
Some cases may result in a partial refund rather than a full refund, including cancellation penalties or used portions of a stay.

## Failed Refunds
If a refund cannot be returned to the original payment method, customer support may request additional payment information or provide an alternative refund process.

## Refund Status
Customers may contact customer support when an approved refund has not appeared after the expected processing period.
